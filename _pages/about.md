<div style="font-size: 1.1em; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 2rem;">
  <p>
    I am <strong>Harihara Maharna</strong>, a PhD Candidate in <strong>Applied and Computational Mathematics</strong> at the 
    <a href="https://acms.nd.edu/">Department of Applied and Computational Mathematics and Statistics (ACMS)</a>, 
    <strong>University of Notre Dame</strong>, IN, USA.
  </p>

<p>
    My research focuses on developing <strong>structure-preserving deep learning methods for complex fluids problems</strong>. I am building the <strong>Energetic Variational</strong> framework — a thermodynamically consistent approach grounded in the Energetic Variational Approach (EnVarA). I work under the supervision of <a href="https://acms.nd.edu/people/zhiliang-xu/">Dr. Zhiliang Xu</a>.
  </p>

  <p>
    Previously, I completed my <strong>MSc in Mathematics</strong> at <a href="https://www.iisertvm.ac.in/">IISER Thiruvananthapuram</a>, India, working on finite volume methods for Euler system under <a href="https://www.iisertvm.ac.in/faculty/arun">Dr. K. R. Arun</a>.
  </p>
</div>
